Questions

What people ask us.

Why is there a monthly fee at all?

Because a website isn't a thing you buy once. It needs hosting, a security certificate, backups and someone to change the hours when they change. The 29 € covers that. We could hide it in a bigger one-off price, but then the site would go stale the moment you paid.

What does 99 € actually get me?

One page — a good one, with every section a local business needs, built for phones. Not a multi-page site, not a shop, not online booking. If you need those, that's Vanguard Pro. We say this plainly because "from 99 €" is worth nothing if you find out later what it excluded.

Really live in five days?

Five working days from the moment we have your content: text, photos, services, hours and legal details. The clock starts there, not at the kick-off call. If material is missing, the clock stops and we tell you what we're waiting for.

I don't have photos of my business.

That's the usual case. We'll give you a guide for shooting them yourself on a phone (it works better than you'd expect) or help you organise a session. What we won't do is use stock photos and pass them off as your premises.

What if I already have a website?

We'll run our checks on it and tell you whether it's worth rebuilding or just fixing. If it's fine, we'll say so, even though that means you don't hire us.

Is the website mine?

Yes. The domain is in your name and the content is yours. Cancel the plan and we hand over the files so you can take it to another provider.

How did you get my details?

From public business sources: open directories, your own website and your public business profiles. We only process professional contact details, never personal data. Tell us to stop and we remove you from our database immediately.

Do you build online shops?

Not as a standard plan. A shop needs payments, stock, shipping and returns, and doing that badly is worse than not doing it. If you need one, it's a Custom project and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right people for it.

Ready to build your digital presence?

Leave us the name of your business. We'll build a draft of your website and show it to you. No cost, no commitment, and nothing for you to write.

What happens next

  1. We look up your business in public sources and run our checks.
  2. We build a draft of your site — a real page, not a mockup image.
  3. We send you a private link. It isn't published and search engines can't see it.
  4. You decide. If it's not for you, we delete it and that's the end of it.
